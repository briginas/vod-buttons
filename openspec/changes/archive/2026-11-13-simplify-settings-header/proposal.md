# Proposal

## Why

The stacked settings heading makes the icon and text look misaligned. The introductory sentence adds unnecessary text before the controls.

## What Changes

- Keep the selected icon on the left and place FOR TWITCH and VOD Buttons on one vertically centered row.
- Remove the introductory replay/skip sentence from settings and the local preview.
- Keep the header on one row at narrow viewport widths.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `appearance`: Specify the compact settings header and removal of the introductory sentence.

## Impact

Settings and preview HTML, shared settings CSS, and the packaged extension. No storage or runtime changes.
