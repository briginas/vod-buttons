Ready when you hover

Open a Twitch VOD and move the pointer over the video. Rewind buttons appear on the left; forward buttons appear on the right. They hide after 1.5 seconds of pointer inactivity. Move the pointer to show them again; keyboard focus keeps them visible. Normal, theater and fullscreen modes are supported.

In a small player, scroll inside a column with your mouse wheel, touchpad gestures or its scrollbar to reach every button. Scrolling the column does not seek the video.

Changes save automatically and update open VODs. Hide a side by removing all its buttons. Live streams, clips and embedded players are not supported.

Only on this device. No account, sync, analytics or viewing history collection.
